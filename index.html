Abstract

This article introduces a novel monocular framework designed to transfer human motions to robotic hands. To address common environmental challenges such as self-occlusion and lighting variations, we propose a shadow-robust feature encoding mechanism that leverages transformer-based attention. A hierarchical constraint solver then combines pose alignment with inter-joint angle optimization, enabling collision-free control for high-DOF robotic hands. The mapping algorithm is further enhanced through adaptive kinematic-structure compensation, effectively resolving structural discrepancies between human and robotic hands to achieve efficient motion transfer. Experimental results demonstrate superior performance in both simulated and physical environments, with a 90\% success rate across manipulation tasks.
